# SpendWise — Student Expense Analyzer

**SpendWise** is a student-focused expense management and analysis web application that helps students track their spending, understand financial habits, manage budgets, and make better financial decisions.

## Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to track daily expenses, identify major spending areas, understand spending patterns, and manage a monthly budget. SpendWise provides a simple platform to record or upload expenses, analyze spending, set financial goals, and receive useful insights.

## Objective

- Track and organize daily expenses.
- Upload monthly expense data in CSV or TXT format.
- Automatically categorize spending.
- Identify major expense areas and spending patterns.
- Manage monthly and category-wise budgets.
- Track savings goals, subscriptions, and recurring expenses.
- Generate financial insights and reports.

## How It Works

```text
Sign In
   ↓
Add Expenses / Upload CSV or TXT
   ↓
Categorize & Process Expenses
   ↓
View Dashboard & Analytics
   ↓
Manage Budget & Savings Goals
   ↓
View Insights, Challenges & Reports

## Features

### Expense Management
- Add expenses with amount, category, date, payment method, and notes.
- Edit and delete transactions.
- Search, filter, and sort expenses.
- Mark expenses as recurring.
- Attach and preview receipts.

### Expense Upload
- Upload monthly expense data in CSV or TXT format.
- Validate uploaded files and expense values.
- Automatically categorize expenses based on expense details.
- Process multiple transactions for analysis.

### Dashboard
- View total spending.
- Track number of transactions.
- Calculate average expense.
- Identify the top spending category.
- View biggest spending areas.
- Monitor monthly spending.
- Access important financial information quickly.

### Analytics
- Category-wise spending analysis.
- Daily and monthly spending trends.
- Weekend vs. weekday spending comparison.
- Identify the highest spending day.
- Interactive **Spending Pulse** visualization.
- Interactive **Money Mix** category breakdown.

### Smart Budget Planner
- Set a monthly budget.
- Create category-wise spending limits.
- Compare actual spending with planned budgets.
- View remaining budget and budget status.
- Identify categories with higher spending.

### Savings Goals
- Create personal savings goals.
- Set target amounts and target dates.
- Track current savings progress.
- Calculate the monthly amount required to reach the goal.

### Subscriptions & Recurring Expenses
- Track regular subscriptions.
- Monitor recurring expenses.
- View recurring spending separately.

### Spending Calendar
- View expenses by individual date.
- Check daily spending totals.
- Identify high-spending days.

### Financial Insights
- Financial Health score based on spending and budget activity.
- **Money Coach** with rule-based financial suggestions.
- **Where Did My Money Go?** breakdown of major spending areas.
- Monthly financial review.
- Spending observations and category insights.

### Student Challenges & Achievements
- Student-focused spending challenges.
- Track challenge progress.
- Earn achievement badges for completing financial activities.

### Reports & Export
- Export expense data as CSV.
- Generate monthly reports.
- Generate category-based reports.
- Review budget and spending information.

### Demo Mode
- Includes sample student expense data.
- Allows users to explore the application without entering their own expenses.
- Useful for demonstrations and testing.

### Authentication
- Sign in using name and Indian mobile number.
- 6-digit demo OTP verification.
- User profile and sign-out functionality.

> **Note:** OTP authentication is browser-based for demonstration purposes and does not send real SMS messages.

## Data & Privacy

- Expense data is stored locally in the browser.
- No real bank or payment account is connected.
- Users can manage, reset, or clear stored application data.
